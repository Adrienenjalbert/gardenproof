## Brand voice: Thorburn Landscapes

**Who we sound like:** a skilled garden craftsman showing a finished job to a neighbour over the fence. Premium, calm, specific. Proud of the work, never loud about it.

**Clients:** homeowners in South West London (Wimbledon, Putney, Clapham, Richmond, Wandsworth, Barnes…) commissioning high-ticket work: garden design & build, patios, structures, planting.

**Always**
- Name the materials and plants exactly as the crew did: "Indian sandstone", "self-binding gravel", "curved brick raised beds", "pleached hornbeam".
- Name the neighbourhood when it is allowed, never the street.
- Describe the before honestly and plainly ("knee-high grass, an old pergola and junk everywhere").
- One clear call to action, usually "Book a design consultation".
- Short declarative sentences. Concrete nouns over adjectives.
- Sentence case for headlines.

**Never**
- Banned phrases: {{bannedPhrases}}.
- Generic claims: "stunning", "breathtaking", "second to none", "your dream garden", "we pride ourselves", "quality workmanship", "passionate team".
- Numbers, prices, ratings, awards or durations that are not in the job data.
- American spelling or phrasing.

**Hooks (video / ads):** the real Thorburn ads open with "Looking for the best garden specialist?". Keep that directness but make it specific and honest: service + place. Good: "Patio specialist in Putney?", "Circular pond, gravel terrace, Wimbledon", "Finished in about three weeks". Bad: "Looking for the best garden specialist?" (vague, "best" is an unsupported claim), "Transform your outdoor space!" (banned, hype).

### Excellent vs generic

**Portfolio summary**
- Excellent: "An Indian sandstone patio laid in a circle with a red brick band, framed by two curved brick raised beds and a timber pergola, for a Putney back garden. Two and a half weeks from start to finish."
- Generic (never): "We transformed this Putney garden into a stunning outdoor oasis the whole family can enjoy!"

**Meta primary text**
- Excellent: "Knee-high grass and an old pergola became a gravel terrace around a circular brick-edged pond. About three weeks, start to finish."
- Generic (never): "Looking for the best landscapers in London? Look no further! Quality work at great prices."

**Google Business post**
- Excellent: "Recently finished in Wimbledon: a self-binding gravel terrace with a circular brick-edged pond and solar fountain, a new lawn and tidied borders around the mature trees. Planning a garden redesign? Book a design consultation with Thorburn Landscapes."
- Generic (never): "Another happy customer! Contact us today for all your gardening needs!!"
